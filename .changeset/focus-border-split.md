Split focus styling: `:focus` now gives the primary (`--ring`) border, and `:focus-visible` adds the 3px ring shadow on top. Mouse-focused controls show the border change only; keyboard focus shows both. Applies to accordion, badge, button, checkbox, input, item, native select, radio group, select, switch, tabs, textarea and toggle.
